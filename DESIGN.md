# MomHaven Design System

## Purpose

MomHaven uses a calm, clinical interface that feels warm without looking decorative or synthetic. Clinical meaning must remain distinct from brand expression.

## Semantic color tokens

- Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (`slate-50`)
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Primary brand: MomHaven purple, used selectively for identity and primary actions
- Primary clinical interactive: `#0F766E` (`teal-700`), hover `#115E59` (`teal-800`)
- Primary text: `#0F172A` (`slate-900`)
- Muted text: `#475569` (`slate-600`)
- Border/divider: `#E2E8F0` (`slate-200`)
- Emergency: `#B91C1C` (`red-700`) on `#FEF2F2` (`red-50`)
- Warning: `#B45309` (`amber-700`) on `#FFFBEB` (`amber-50`)
- Normal: `#15803D` (`green-700`) on `#F0FDF4` (`green-50`)

Clinical status colors communicate status only; they are not interchangeable with the MomHaven brand color.

## Typography

- Headings: DM Sans, 600/700
- Clinical values, measurements, dates and identifiers: DM Mono where available
- Body: Inter, with system fallbacks

## Surfaces and layout

- Standard radius: `6px` / Tailwind `rounded-md`
- Standard elevation: `shadow-sm` only
- Standard divider: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
- Prefer dense, structured information over decorative cards.
- Avoid glassmorphism, translucent cards, blur effects, and oversized rounded containers.
- Avoid colored top/left borders as decorative accents.
- Avoid gradients for routine clinical surfaces.
- Avoid floating announcement pills and generic marketing CTAs.
- Use explicit actions such as `Record ANC Visit`, `View reminders`, or `Choose active context`.

## Brand usage

MomHaven purple remains the brand identifier. It may be used for logos, selected primary controls, and identity moments, but it must not replace semantic clinical colors.

## Clinical provenance

MOH Kenya handbook material is a content and clinical-governance source. It must not be represented as prescribing this visual design system unless the source explicitly does so.
